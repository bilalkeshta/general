Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the information collected by us, through our mobile applications and how we use that information.

We do not collect any personal information with our mobile applications. When you download and use our mobile applications, we don't require you to provide any information and we don't collect any information about you. Except in some cases, we collect your device ID to show you personalised advertisements. In addition, we might collect non-personal information about app usage. We use this data to inform improvements we can make to our applications.

Our mobile applications comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. We don't knowingly collect personal information of any kind from users of any age.

Users may contact us via email if they have questions about our mobile applications. We may receive a user's email address and other information about a user if the user submits that information to us through email. We will only use this information to respond to any inquiries from the user.

Please feel free to contact us with any questions about our privacy policy and practices.
