Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the information collected by us through our mobile applications and how we use that information.

We do not require users to provide personal information when downloading or using our mobile applications. In some cases, device identifiers may be used for advertising purposes. We may also collect non-personal information about app usage to understand how our applications are used and to help us improve them.

Our mobile applications are designed with children's privacy in mind. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Our applications may use third-party services, including advertising and analytics services. These third-party services may collect certain information according to their own privacy policies.

If users contact us by email, we may receive their email address and any other information they voluntarily provide. This information will only be used to respond to their inquiries or provide requested support.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through our support email.

Our mobile applications comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. We don't knowingly collect personal information of any kind from users of any age.

Users may contact us via email if they have questions about our mobile applications. We may receive a user's email address and other information about a user if the user submits that information to us through email. We will only use this information to respond to any inquiries from the user.

Please feel free to contact us with any questions about our privacy policy and practices.
